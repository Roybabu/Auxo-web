PURPOSE AND STATUS RULE
-----------------------
This checklist describes what remains to reach the relevant AUXO Android business
functionality in the web app. “Implemented” means a web workflow or business
service exists. It does not certify exact Android parity; those workflows still
need side-by-side comparison against Android temp for calculations, validations,
edge cases, and database relationships.
